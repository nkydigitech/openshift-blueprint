## Who Is This For?
Global beginners to enterprise teams — DevOps students, K8s users moving to enterprise, platform engineers. Zero OpenShift experience needed. Global analogies only.

## The Global Analogy System (Easy Grab)
- **Kubernetes = Engine, OpenShift = Full Car with Airbags, Insurance & Dashboard** — K8s is powerful but bare, OpenShift adds safety and comfort for enterprise
- **OKD = Community Edition Car** — same engine, community-maintained
- **Pod = Apartment** where your app lives
- **Node = Building** that holds many apartments
- **Cluster = Estate** with many buildings
- **Project = Gated Compound** — isolated area with own rules
- **Route = Reception Gate** that directs visitors to the right apartment
- **S2I (Source-to-Image) = Magic Kitchen** — turns raw recipe (code) into ready meal (container image) without writing Dockerfile
- **BuildConfig = Bakery Order Form** — tells how to bake the image
- **ImageStream = Photo Album** tracking all versions of your meal
- **Template = House Blueprint** — reusable plan for entire app stack
- **Operator = Robot Manager** — knows how to install, backup, upgrade complex apps automatically
